# Expressive Clock v0.1.5

A major refinement of World Clock, more dependable alarms and widgets, and layouts that make better use of phones and foldables.

## Home-screen widgets

- Digital widget time and date use Android’s native live clock views, so they continue updating without opening the app or widget settings. Analogue widgets retain native clock hands.
- Improved widget recovery after boot, time/locale changes, resizing and launcher updates; rendering failures fall back to a working native clock.
- Digital previews now match the native font profile available on the device. Condensed and Flex preferences choose verified native Google Sans Flex where available, with disclosed system-font fallbacks on other devices.
- Better fitting for narrow widgets, seconds, stacked layouts and long dates. Widget preferences remain saved independently for each widget.

## World Clock redesign

- World Clock has its own bottom-navigation destination, with a large analogue clock above a compact, responsive two-column city grid.
- Refined city cards feature native curved wave artwork, soft tonal gradients, luminous edges, gentle elevation and theme-aware selected outlines/glows. Light, dark and pure-black themes use the same visual system.
- Explicit bundled Google Sans Flex typography gives city names, dates and time digits consistent proportions. Card times use tabular alignment and mechanical digit updates.
- Small golden sun and blue-grey moon badges use approximate solar conditions at representative city coordinates, with an offline fallback for locations without coordinates.
- Selecting a city smoothly moves the analogue hands to its timezone. The selected city name uses staggered mechanical character reels with progressive blur, including longer and multi-word names. Timezone descriptions cross-dissolve.
- Scrolling transforms the analogue presentation into a compact digital clock. The clocks use a deliberate opacity handover so they never appear together. Releases snap to expanded or collapsed positions, with velocity-aware flick handling.
- The compact digital readout shares Timer’s rounded numeral renderer, with theme-coloured minutes/seconds, sequential ticking, blurred edges and full-display mechanical transitions when changing cities.
- Add Location opens as a compact adaptive sheet with offline suggested cities, filled search, clear action, word-capitalisation keyboard hints and an empty-results state. Search focus expands the sheet around the keyboard; existing locations are excluded and duplicates are prevented in storage.
- Newly added locations reveal and scroll into view with coordinated card/glow motion. Fixed viewport falloffs avoid moving blur bands on unfolded displays.
- Long-press a saved location to select locations for deletion. Selected cards lift while unselected cards dim; confirmation and accessibility delete actions remain available. Remaining cards glide into place with the Alarm cards’ spring motion. The active location outline is visually distinct from deletion checkmarks.

## Alarms and alarm reliability

- Shared alarm-stream audio playback now handles transient audio focus, asynchronous preparation, stale callbacks and cleanup consistently for alarms and previews.
- Custom audio retains document read access. Missing or unreadable audio tries the system alarm sound and then a bundled audible fallback; explicit Silent remains intentional.
- Atomic alarm edits and occurrence validation reject stale callbacks, preserve dismissed occurrences during label/sound-only edits and retain recurring schedules. Re-arming or changing the time/repeat rule resets the consumed occurrence appropriately.
- A one-shot alarm disarms when it fires, and snoozing re-arms its snoozed occurrence. Timer expiry rejects paused and premature callbacks.
- Serialized ringer starts prevent a stale/deleted alarm from replacing or stopping an active alarm.
- Ringing notifications retain open, Snooze and Dismiss controls. On Android versions that permit swiping an ongoing notification away, that dismissal now stops the ringing service instead of leaving inaccessible audio running. The finished-timer notification has corresponding stop-on-dismiss behaviour.
- Saving an enabled alarm confirms the approximate time until it rings. This is a save confirmation, not a persistent countdown on every saved alarm card.

## Upcoming alarm notifications

- A separate quiet notification appears approximately 30 minutes before an alarm, using AlarmManager events rather than polling.
- “Dismiss this occurrence” skips only the upcoming occurrence of a recurring alarm and preserves its repeat days and future schedule. A dismissed one-shot alarm is disabled.
- Stale notification actions are checked against the scheduled occurrence. These upcoming notices are separate from notifications shown while an alarm is actively ringing.

## Alarm sound selection

- New and existing alarms share a full-screen Alarm Sound library with a clear header, Back and Apply actions.
- A compact selected-sound card offers Play/Pause preview and a restrained playback visualisation. Your Audio, Device Sounds, Default and Silent choices are grouped clearly.
- Choose Audio File uses Android’s document picker and persisted access. Preview resources and audio focus are released on exit/backgrounding.
- Apply updates the alarm editor draft; leaving without applying preserves the previous selection. The page includes horizontal/depth motion and predictive-back progress/cancellation handling where supported.

## Timer and stopwatch

- Long-press any Timer preset to open that exact preset’s existing minutes/seconds editor and quick options. Set saves it directly; Cancel preserves it. Normal taps still start timers immediately.
- Timer readouts, ring clearance, wrapping presets and compact controls adapt more safely to enlarged display/font settings and short windows.
- Stopwatch gains the same restrained ambient clock lighting. Its dial remains circular and controls fit more reliably, including short labels and tighter button spacing when needed.
- Timer deadlines and stopwatch elapsed time continue to use elapsed realtime rather than UI ticks.

## Foldable and responsive layouts

- Alarm and World Clock remain distinct destinations when unfolded.
- The wide Alarm page keeps a two-column alarm-card grid on the left, with the local analogue clock, matching mechanical digital readout and date on the right.
- The wide World Clock page places the selected clock/details on the left and the city grid on the right.
- A persistent shared analogue clock glides between these positions while its hands keep updating; timezone changes animate rather than reset the clock.
- Layout selection follows available width/height, with phone layouts retained in narrower windows.
- Improved dock clearance, sheet dimensions, fixed scroll-edge padding, responsive title fitting and persistent progressive falloffs protect against enlarged Android display/font settings without globally disabling accessibility scaling.
- New/Edit Alarm headers stay visible around the keyboard; alarm names retain typed text. AM/PM controls, weekday strips, switches and long sound names fit more reliably.

## Navigation, settings and animations

- World Clock is available as a default startup page, persisted through the existing settings store. Direct alarm/timer/notification navigation still takes priority.
- Theme-aware system-bar styling fixes dark status-bar icons on dark backgrounds.
- Refined four-icon dock spacing, globe outline weight and sphere rotation, with Australia as the starting region and fewer rendering seams.
- The Stopwatch navigation animation rotates the inner hand; Timer retains its outline rotation.
- Shared sheet presentation improves entrance/dismissal on wider windows. Reduced-motion handling is retained for the mechanical clock and globe animations.
- Clock dates/timezone labels now observe configuration locale changes correctly.

## Additional bug fixes and development tooling

- Fixed city-grid animation state races during addition and placement suppression during deletion.
- Refined ambient lighting bounds and card entrance coordination to reduce clipped or flashing glows.
- Fixed release lint API declarations/resources for older supported Android versions and documented the required pre-Android-14 quick-settings launch path.
- Added focused regression coverage for audio lifecycle, occurrences, startup routing, clock geometry, solar cues, mechanical text and location choices.
- A separate Expressive Clock Installer Helper and opt-in Actions/ntfy delivery tooling are included in source. The helper is an independent app and is not bundled in the production APK/AAB. Its authentication and delivery configuration are separate from the main app.

## Known limitations

- After a device reboot, alarms are rescheduled only after the first unlock. Alarm delivery before that unlock is not implemented. Unlock the device after restarting it before relying on alarms.
- Timer and stopwatch state uses elapsed realtime; continuity across a full device reboot is not guaranteed.
- System alarm volume, DND policy, notification/full-screen-intent permissions and OEM power restrictions still apply. Explicit Silent sounds remain silent.
- Widget font families can differ by OEM; disclosed native fallbacks prioritise accurate live time over matching the bundled app font exactly.
- Solar badges are approximate visual cues, not precise sunrise/sunset forecasts.
- Responsive improvements were tested on Pixel Fold and OPPO Find N6 configurations; this does not establish a fix on every device, including the OPPO Find X9 Pro and Galaxy S26 reported in issue #3. Extreme scaling, older Android releases and every launcher remain outside the complete device matrix.
- Automatic Dynamic Island hiding for fullscreen/landscape apps (issue #7) and a persistent countdown on saved alarm cards (issue #10) remain open requests.
- The standalone GitHub APK uses the same signing certificate as v0.1.4’s GitHub APK. Updating a Google Play installation is supported only if its Play App Signing certificate matches; the upload certificate alone does not prove this.
